### 1. **What does the system do and why would a user want to use it?**

**DUET** connects users through shared musical interests by analyzing their Spotify activity to match them with similar tastes. The platform recommends local concerts and events, encouraging users to attend together, fostering friendships and community. Unlike dating platforms, DUET focuses on platonic connections centered around music. Whether users want to expand their social circle or meet like-minded people, DUET provides a fun and natural way to connect through music.


### 2. **How to run the software?**

Go to the link provided here: https://karlabelem.github.io/duet.github.io/

### 3. **How to use the software?**

- **Create your profile**: In the log in screen, click on “register” if you don’t have an account, and go through the steps. DO NOT USE REAL EMAILS AND PASSWORDS: This beta release isn’t secure yet. 
- **Customize your profile**: Once logged in, you can edit your profile in the user profile screen.
- **Match with Users**: Once logged in, DUET will match you with users who have similar music tastes.
- **Messaging**: Chat with users through the integrated real-time messaging system.

### 4. **How to report a bug?**

To report bugs:
1. Go to the **Issue Tracker** on GitHub: [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/your-repo-url/issues).
2. Click "New Issue" and fill out the provided template:
   - **Title**: Brief bug description.
   - **Steps to Reproduce**: Describe how to reproduce the issue.
   - **Expected Behavior**: What should have happened.
   - **Actual Behavior**: What happened instead.
   - **Environment**: Include browser details and any other relevant info.

### 5. **Known bugs?**

- **Spotify Authentication**: Users may experience rate-limiting issues when authenticating with Spotify during high traffic times.
- **No security with database**: Database isn’t secure since writes to it are public. Don’t use private information yet.
